


# Gladys Wanyoike Portfolio

## Overview

This is a **personal portfolio website** for me (Gladys Wanyoike), a Communications and Public Relations student. The website showcases her **professional profile**, **skills**, **services**, **education**, **experience**, and provides a way for potential clients or collaborators to get in touch. The portfolio is **responsive, modern, and visually appealing**, built using **HTML5, CSS3, Bootstrap 5, and AOS for animations**.

---

## Features

* **Hero Section:**

  * Welcoming introduction with Gladys’ name, professional roles, and social links.
  * Interactive typing effect showing multiple roles (Designer, Team Leader, Academic Writer & Researcher, Photographer).

* **About Section:**

  * Professional bio and career overview.
  * Profile card with photo, experience stats, rating, and social media links.
  * Detailed contact information including email, phone, and location.

* **Services Section:**

  * Highlights core services Gladys offers, including:

    * Brand Identity
    * Public Relations
    * Video Editing
    * Social Media Management
  * Option to expand services for other offerings.

* **Resume / Education Section:**

  * Experience timeline showcasing professional roles and freelance projects.
  * Academic background including university degree and previous education.

* **Contact Section:**

  * Fully functional contact form for inquiries or project requests.
  * Provides email and phone as alternative contact methods.

* **Footer:**

  * Includes useful links, social media links, and contact info.
  * Designed with copyright and credits.

---

## Technologies Used

* **HTML5 & CSS3** – Semantic structure and styling.
* **Bootstrap 5** – Responsive grid system and components.
* **AOS (Animate On Scroll)** – Smooth scroll animations.
* **Bootstrap Icons** – Professional iconography.
* **JavaScript (Typed.js)** – Interactive typing effect on the Hero section.
